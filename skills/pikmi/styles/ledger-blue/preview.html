Receivables

Reminders go out on their own

Customers hear from you before the due date, after it, and once more when they pay.

Due in 3 daysA short reminder with the invoice attached
OverdueA second note, copied to the account owner
PaidA receipt, and the reminders stop

Done means zero

One number sits at the top of every close: what is left to reconcile. Each matched line brings it down, and the report unlocks when it reads 0.00.
