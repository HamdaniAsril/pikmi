A walled garden behind the old orchard house

The orchard house sits at the end of Merrow Lane, past the last row of apple trees. Behind it is a rose garden with a brick wall all the way round and a pergola down the middle. That is where we will say our vows.

Arif's grandmother planted the first roses by the gate. They still flower every June, which is how we chose the fourteenth.

The shuttle times and your table are on your guest page.

How the day unfolds

Everything happens in the garden or a few steps from it. Bring flat shoes: the lawn is soft.

  1. 11:00

    The vows

    Under the rose arch. Chairs are set out on the lawn, so come a little early.

  2. 12:30

    Lunch under the pergola

    Long tables, a garden menu and a vegetarian one. Your table is on the card at the gate.

  3. 15:00

    Tea in the orchard

    Cake, lawn games, and a slow walk through the apple trees for anyone who wants one.

  4. 19:00

    Music on the terrace

    A small band plays until late. The lanterns go up when the light goes gold.

“Come as you are, and stay until the candles burn down.”
