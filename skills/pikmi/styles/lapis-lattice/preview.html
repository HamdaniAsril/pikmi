The square

What waits in the square

Kitchens line the library side, drinks and crafts close the ring, and the courtyard in the middle belongs to the children.

Twenty kitchens

Satay, martabak, grilled corn and rice plates, cooked by the families who live around the square.

Stalls 1 to 20

The kids' corner

Lantern making and storytelling in the courtyard, every night until nine.

Free, no sign-up needed

  • Bring a container

    Every stall fills your own box or bottle. There are no plastic bags in the square.

  • Pay at the stall

    Cash or a phone payment, straight to the family that cooked.

  • Leave by the main road

    The library side closes first, so the last walk out is past the drink stalls.
