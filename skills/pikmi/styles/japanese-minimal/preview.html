A small shop for three teas and the time to drink them.

Kiriha keeps only sencha, hojicha and matcha. Each comes from one farm and is packed in small bags, so it is still fresh when you open it.

We do three things, slowly

Steam for thirty seconds

Our sencha is steamed only briefly, so it stays green and a little grassy.

Pack in small bags

Fifty grams at most. A bag is finished in two weeks, before the tea fades.
